# Phase 1 HUD, notifications and floor timers (022)

Apply `supabase/migrations/202611270022_hud_notifications.sql` after migration
021, then reload the game. No environment variables or provider setup are needed.
The migration is additive and does not rewrite crawlers, inventory, account
writeups, point balances, Race/Class benefits or selection deliveries. It starts
no timers and sends no historical alerts. Existing crawlers need no reconciliation.

## GM workflow

- Open a game. The HUD sits above the existing character sheet; crawler selection
  and the existing GM/encounter/selection controls remain available.
- Click **Broadcast**. Choose All Crawlers or a named crawler, a category, title
  and message, then **Send alert**. The GM receives a copy. All Crawlers delivers
  to current crawler owners; joining/creating a crawler later does not expose
  earlier private history. Broadcasts are limited to 20 per minute per game.
- **Notifications** opens the durable history. Read acknowledgements are private
  to each account. Mark All Read covers the loaded snapshot's watermark, so alerts
  arriving during acknowledgement remain unread. Older Alerts pages through the
  whole campaign log that your account is authorized to see.
- Click **Floor Collapse** to manage the selected crawler's current floor timer.
  Enter days/hours/minutes and Save Paused or Save & Start. Pause, Resume and Clear
  remain available without closing the drawer after your own successful save.
  A competing GM session's change rejects a stale edit; close and reopen controls.

Each floor (1–18) has its own timer, up to 365 days. Pausing freezes the server's
remaining duration; running timers use a shared server deadline. A timer reaching
zero displays an expired reminder. The GM resolves collapse: no scheduled damage,
automatic floor movement or offline jobs are introduced. Clearing pauses at zero.

## Player workflow and live behavior

Players see the timer and their private alert log; they cannot broadcast or change
timers. Incoming live alerts briefly show a dismissible banner without moving
focus. Achievement-tagged messages use the gold NEW ACHIEVEMENT UNLOCKED banner.
This is a GM-authored announcement in Phase 1, not a new achievement/reward record.
Those records and actual reward grants arrive in Phase 2.

The HUD reads independently of the existing guarded sheet refresh. It can update
while a player edits a form or uses an open sheet dialog, without replacing that
form. Modern browsers put banners in a nonmodal top-layer popover; older browsers
retain fixed-position banners. At most three fresh live banners are queued, each
lasting about 6.5 seconds; all alerts remain in the log. Initial loads, reconnects
and periodic recovery restore history silently rather than replaying old banners.
An interrupted connection retries HUD reads every 15 seconds while the page is
visible. The application uses the existing private, payload-free live channel.

Automatic alerts are generated by successful database operations only:

- A GM changes a positive saferoom stat-point authorization. Identical balances,
  floor-reset closures and historical data do not spam notifications.
- The GM resolves a marked Skill's advancement attempt, including a held rank.
  Marking/unmarking alone is not a resolved advancement.
- An official Tutorial crawler crosses from at least 30% to below 30% Health.
  Staying below the threshold creates no additional alert. Recovering to 30% or
  more allows a new alert on a subsequent crossing. The owner and GM receive it.

Alerts do not award stats, loot or achievements, and do not call Groq. Lore,
private inventory, other crawlers' detailed sheets and future fog-of-war data are
not broadcast. Crawler Menus and Map are reserved for the approved later phases.
Safe Room/Loot is enabled by migration 023; see `SAFE_ROOM_SETUP.md`.

## Hosted GM/player acceptance

Use the GM plus two player accounts in separate browser sessions after applying
022. Do not run the isolated fixture suites against the hosted game database.

1. Open the same game in all sessions. Confirm the sheet, inventory, Hotlist,
   encounter and pushed selection controls still work and existing values persist.
2. Send an All Crawlers alert. Both current crawler owners and the GM should
   receive it. Reload and verify the log persists without replaying its banner.
3. Target player A. Player B must see neither its text nor its unread count.
   Mark it read as A and confirm the GM's copy remains unread.
4. Send an Achievement alert. Confirm its gold banner and permanent log entry.
   No inventory or achievement reward should be created by that announcement.
5. Leave a sheet edit unfinished while another session sends an alert. Confirm
   the badge/banner updates, focus stays in the edit and the draft is unchanged.
   Repeat inside a sheet dialog. Finish/cancel normally to release sheet refresh.
6. Exercise newest/older history and Mark All Read. Send an alert from another
   session while acknowledging; a later arrival must remain unread.
7. Set a short timer as GM, pause/resume it, refresh a player session and compare
   displays. Change the selected crawler/floor and verify timers are separate.
   Compete with an old GM form: the stale edit must fail, preserving newer state.
8. Let a short timer expire. Confirm it stays at zero and does not move floors,
   apply damage or change resources. Players must have no timer-edit controls.
9. Exercise a real authorized stat-point change and a marked Skill advancement.
   Check exactly one owner/GM alert and unchanged grants beyond the original
   operation. Unmarking alone must not impersonate an advancement resolution.
10. With table-approved damage/healing, cross from 30% to 20% Health, stay low,
    recover and cross again. Expect one alert per downward threshold crossing.
11. Check Light/Dark mode, keyboard close/focus behavior, phone width and reconnect
    recovery. No automatic AI requests should be made by this feature.

bounded history, request idempotence, rate limits, floor timer versions and all

authenticated multi-account checklist above documents the original acceptance
workflow. On November 27, 2026, the user confirmed migration 022 applied and tests
